Privacy Policy

ABC Jungle

Effective Date: February 2025

Introduction

ABC Jungle ("we", "our", or "the App") is a children's educational application designed for kids ages 2-4. We are committed to protecting the privacy of children and their families. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information in connection with our App.

Children's Privacy

ABC Jungle is designed for use by children under 13. We comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and applicable child privacy laws. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Information We Collect

ABC Jungle does not collect, store, or share any personal information from its users. Specifically:

  • We do not require account creation or login.
  • We do not collect names, email addresses, or any personally identifiable information.
  • We do not track location data.
  • We do not use analytics that identify individual users.

Advertising

The free version of ABC Jungle displays ads provided by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect certain device information for the purpose of serving ads. We have configured AdMob to serve child-directed ads in compliance with COPPA. No personalized advertising is shown to users.

Users can remove all ads by purchasing the premium version of the App through an in-app purchase.

In-App Purchases

ABC Jungle offers a one-time in-app purchase to unlock premium features and remove ads. All purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not have access to any payment information.

Third-Party Services

The App uses the following third-party services:

  • Google AdMob — for displaying advertisements (free version only). Google Privacy Policy
  • Apple StoreKit — for processing in-app purchases. Apple Privacy Policy

Data Storage

All app preferences (such as sound settings) are stored locally on the device and are not transmitted to any server.

Parental Rights

Since we do not collect personal information, there is no personal data to review, modify, or delete. If you believe we have inadvertently collected information from a child, please contact us immediately and we will take steps to remove it.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
