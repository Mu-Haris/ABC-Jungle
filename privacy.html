Privacy Policy

ABC Jungle

Effective Date: September 2026

Introduction

ABC Jungle ("we", "our", or "the App") is an educational application for early learners. This Privacy Policy explains what information is collected in connection with the App, how it is used, and the choices available to you.

Who the App Is For

ABC Jungle is a general-audience application. It is not directed to children under 13, and it is not distributed in the App Store Kids category. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, please contact us at the address below and we will delete it.

Information We Collect Directly

We do not operate servers and we do not collect personal information ourselves. Specifically:

  • No account, login, or registration is required.
  • We do not ask for names, email addresses, or other personally identifiable information.
  • App preferences, such as sound settings and whether you have purchased the ad-free version, are stored locally on your device.

Advertising and Information Collected by Google AdMob

The free version of ABC Jungle displays advertisements provided by Google AdMob. To serve and measure those ads, AdMob and its advertising partners may collect and process:

  • Device identifiers, including the Identifier for Advertisers (IDFA)
  • Advertising data, such as which ads were shown, viewed, or tapped
  • Product interaction data, such as app open and usage events
  • Coarse location, derived from your IP address (we do not collect precise GPS location)
  • Diagnostic data, such as crash and performance information

Where you have given permission, this information may be used to show you personalised advertising and to measure its performance across apps and websites owned by other companies. This constitutes "tracking" as Apple defines it.

Google's use of this information is governed by the Google Privacy & Terms and by Google's list of advertising partners.

Your Choices and How to Change Them

You are in control of this at all times:

  • App Tracking Transparency. When you first open the App, iOS asks whether ABC Jungle may track you across apps and websites. If you decline, the IDFA is not made available and advertising is not personalised. You can change this at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Consent (EEA, UK and other regulated regions). Where required by law, a consent form is shown when you first open the App, listing the purposes and the advertising partners involved. You can review or withdraw that consent at any time using the Privacy Options button in the App's Settings screen.
  • Remove ads entirely. A one-time in-app purchase removes all advertising from the App. No ad requests are made once it is active.

In-App Purchases

ABC Jungle offers a single, one-time in-app purchase that removes ads and unlocks additional content. All purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. We never see or receive your payment details.

Third-Party Services

  • Google AdMob — advertising in the free version. Google Privacy Policy
  • Google User Messaging Platform — collecting and recording your consent choice where required.
  • Apple StoreKit — processing in-app purchases. Apple Privacy Policy

Data Retention

Because we do not operate servers, we hold no personal data to retain or delete. Information processed by Google for advertising is retained according to Google's own retention policies, linked above.

Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete personal data held about you, and to object to or restrict its processing. As we hold no such data ourselves, requests relating to advertising data should be directed to Google using the links above. You may also contact us and we will help where we can.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
